Style

Filters applied to the image, top to bottom, before its edges are found. Tick to use; ↑ ↓ to change the order. Painterly calms texture, so the edges left are the forms'.

Canvas

Height 0 follows the image. A fixed height crops the image to cover the drawing (it is never stretched); a size preset turns to the image's orientation.

Edges & detail

All lengths are on the drawing. The Edges view shows what is found, before it is joined into one line.

Smoothing

The line drives along the traced edges at constant speed, aiming the look-ahead distance ahead, never turning tighter than the smallest bend: no corner can be tight, it strays instead.

Takes out every wiggle shorter than the length scale, the same everywhere. It does not hold to the smallest bend: a longer scale leaves fewer tight places.

Moves every point that bends tighter than the smallest bend toward its neighbours, within the stray. Needs a smallest bend above 0.

Experimental smoothing

These act only with a smallest bend above 0 (Fewer bends works without one); merge and cut keep within the stray above. The figures show how many places stay tight and how far the line strays from the edges.
